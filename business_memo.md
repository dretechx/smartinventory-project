# QuickMart Inventory Recommendation Memo

To: QuickMart Management Team

From: Drequan Walker

Subject: SmartInventory Sales Analysis Recommendations

## Business Problem

QuickMart currently experiences stock shortages because inventory decisions rely on fixed reorder schedules rather than actual sales behavior. This causes empty shelves, lost sales, and customer dissatisfaction.

## Analysis Summary

A 90-day sales simulation was conducted using generated product and sales data across multiple categories.

Key findings include:

- Weekend sales were significantly higher than weekday sales.
- Several products repeatedly ranked among top sellers.
- Certain categories showed stronger and more consistent demand patterns.
- Demand spikes create inventory risks.

## Recommendations

1. Increase inventory levels for top-selling products before weekends.

2. Replace fixed reorder schedules with sales-pattern-based forecasting.

3. Monitor product demand daily for high-volume items.

4. Increase stock during high-demand periods and holidays.

## Expected Impact

Implementing these recommendations can reduce stock shortages, improve customer satisfaction, and reduce lost sales.
